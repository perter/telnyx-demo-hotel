## TelnyxHotel Rome Trastevere — About the Hotel
TelnyxHotel Rome Trastevere is a relaxed, family-friendly hotel of 68 rooms in a former convent in Trastevere, the lively old quarter on the west bank of the Tiber. ## TelnyxHotel Rome Trastevere — Location and Getting There
TelnyxHotel Rome Trastevere is on a quiet lane at the foot of the Gianicolo hill, about five minutes on foot from Piazza di Santa Maria in Trastevere. Tram 8 stops on Viale di Trastevere, about seven minutes on foot, and reaches Largo di Torre Argentina and Piazza Venezia in the historic centre in about ten minutes. Osteria del Chiostro Verde is the restaurant of TelnyxHotel Rome Trastevere, a casual trattoria with tables in the cloister. ## TelnyxHotel Rome Trastevere — Nearby Sights and Neighbourhood Tips
The basilica of Santa Maria in Trastevere, with its golden mosaics, is about five minutes on foot from TelnyxHotel Rome Trastevere.
