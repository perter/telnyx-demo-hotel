## TelnyxHotel Chicago Riverwalk — About the Hotel
TelnyxHotel Chicago Riverwalk is a 214-room city hotel in a converted 1920s office tower of 22 floors, standing beside the Chicago River where the Loop meets River North. The style is industrial art deco: brass lift doors, terrazzo floors, exposed steel beams and large factory-style windows. It is the largest and most business-oriented TelnyxHotel, popular with conference guests during the week and with architecture lovers at weekends.

## TelnyxHotel Chicago Riverwalk — Location and Getting There
TelnyxHotel Chicago Riverwalk sits on a side street a few steps from the Chicago Riverwalk promenade, between the Loop and River North. The nearest elevated train stations are State/Lake and Clark/Lake, both about five minutes on foot, served by most of the Loop lines.

From O'Hare International Airport, a taxi takes about 40 to 50 minutes depending on traffic. The Blue Line train runs from O'Hare to Clark/Lake in about 45 minutes, followed by a five-minute walk. From Midway International Airport, a taxi takes about 30 minutes, and the Orange Line train reaches the Loop in about 30 minutes. Union Station, for long-distance and commuter trains, is about 20 minutes on foot or under 10 minutes by taxi. Taxis and ride-hailing cars stop at the covered entrance of TelnyxHotel Chicago Riverwalk, and the front desk can order one at any hour.

## TelnyxHotel Chicago Riverwalk — Nearby Sights and Neighbourhood Tips
The Chicago Riverwalk begins a few steps from TelnyxHotel Chicago Riverwalk and is pleasant for a morning walk or run. Architecture boat tours leave from docks a few minutes away and are the classic way to see the skyline.
