## TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore — About the Hotel
TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore is a calm, wellness-minded hotel of 96 rooms in a slim mid-century building of 14 floors in the Gold Coast, one block back from Lake Michigan. The interiors are light and quiet: pale oak, linen, sand and soft blue tones, with lake light in most public rooms. Guests choose TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore for its indoor pool and spa, its closeness to the beach and the lakefront trail, and its residential, leafy surroundings.

TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore is a fictional hotel created for a technology demo. It has no official star rating and is not part of any real hotel group.

## TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore — Location and Getting There
TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore stands on a tree-lined residential street in the Gold Coast, about five minutes on foot from Oak Street Beach and the Lakefront Trail. The nearest train station is Clark/Division on the Red Line, about ten minutes on foot; the Red Line reaches the Loop in about ten minutes.

From O'Hare International Airport, a taxi takes about 40 to 50 minutes. By train, guests take the Blue Line into the Loop and change to the Red Line, about 70 minutes in total. From Midway International Airport, a taxi takes about 35 to 40 minutes, or guests can take the Orange Line to the Loop and change to the Red Line, about an hour in total. Union Station is about 15 minutes by taxi. Taxis and ride-hailing cars can pull up directly in front of TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore, and the front desk will call one on request.

## TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore — Room Categories
There are four room categories at TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore. The Essential Room is about 20 square metres, with a queen bed and a courtyard view onto the hotel's planted inner garden, for up to 2 guests. The Deluxe City View is about 28 square metres, with a king bed and a sofa bed and a city view over the Gold Coast rooftops, in some rooms with a glimpse of the lake, for up to 3 guests.

The Family Studio is about 38 square metres, made of two connecting rooms with a kitchenette, for up to 4 guests. The Skyline Suite is about 55 square metres on the top floor, with a private terrace facing the lake and the skyline, for up to 4 guests. No room at TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore takes more than 4 guests, and children count as guests. Room prices are given by the booking system, not in this document.

## TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore — Breakfast
The breakfast buffet at TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore is included in every rate and is served from 7:30 to 10:30 daily in the Pale Heron restaurant on the ground floor. It leans towards lighter food: fresh juices, a fruit and granola bar, overnight oats, smoked fish, eggs to order, avocado toast and wholegrain breads, alongside pastries and pancakes. Plant-based milks and gluten-free breads are always available.

## TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore — Restaurants and Bars
The Pale Heron is the restaurant of TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore. It serves a vegetable-forward menu with lake fish and a few meat dishes, open for lunch from 12:00 to 14:30 and for dinner from 18:00 to 21:30. It is closed for dinner on Sundays.

The Driftglass Lounge on the first floor is a quiet bar with a fireplace, open from 15:00 to 23:00, offering wines by the glass, low-alcohol cocktails and a large tea list. Room service at TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore is available from 7:00 to 22:00.

## TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore — Fitness, Wellness and Pool
TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore has an indoor heated lap pool of 20 metres on the second floor, open daily from 6:00 to 22:00 and free for hotel guests. Children under 16 may use the pool until 18:00 with an adult. Next to the pool is the Lakemist Spa, with a sauna, a steam room and four treatment rooms offering massages and facials; treatments are charged separately and should be booked a day ahead. The sauna and steam room are free for guests aged 16 and over.

The fitness studio is open 24 hours with the room key and has cardio machines, weights and stationary bikes. TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore offers a free guided run along the lakefront at 6:45 on weekday mornings and a free yoga class at 8:00 on Saturdays and Sundays.

## TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore — Families and Children
TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore suits families who like the beach and the parks. The Family Studio sleeps up to 4 guests, children included, and its kitchenette is handy for preparing simple meals. Cots, high chairs and bottle warmers are free on request. Family swim time in the pool is from 9:00 to 18:00. The front desk lends beach toys, buckets and a beach umbrella in summer. ## TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore — Pets
TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore is a very dog-friendly hotel. Up to two dogs of any size are welcome per room, with no supplement. A bed, bowls and treats are provided, and the front desk has maps of dog-friendly lakefront walks and a nearby dog beach. Dogs are welcome in the Driftglass Lounge and the lobby but not in the Pale Heron, the pool or the spa. Cats are accepted on request. Assistance animals are welcome everywhere.

## TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore — Parking
TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore has a small underground garage of 30 spaces, reached by a ramp beside the entrance. Guests park their own cars; a parking charge applies per night, and the front desk confirms the rate. Spaces cannot be guaranteed without a reservation, so guests should request one when booking. Two spaces have electric-vehicle chargers.

## TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore — Accessibility
The entrance of TelnyxHotel Chicago Lakeshore is step-free, and two lifts serve every floor, including the pool level and the top floor. Six accessible rooms are available with wide doors, roll-in showers and grab bars. The pool has a hoist for guests with reduced mobility, and the spa has an accessible changing room. The Pale Heron and the Driftglass Lounge are wheelchair accessible.
