## TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring — About the Hotel
TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring is the smallest TelnyxHotel, with 38 rooms spread across five adjoining 17th-century canal houses on the western canal ring of Amsterdam. The houses have gabled façades, tall windows, steep staircases and a shared hidden garden at the back. TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring has its own jetty with a small electric boat, a fleet of bicycles and a strong focus on sustainability. It is quiet, personal and best for couples and independent travellers.

TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring is a fictional hotel invented for a technology demo. It has no official star rating and no connection with any real hotel or group.

## TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring — Location and Getting There
TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring faces one of the main canals of the western canal ring, a few steps from the Nine Streets shopping lanes and about five minutes on foot from the Westerkerk. Trams stop at Westermarkt, about five minutes on foot, and at Dam Square, about ten minutes away.

From Amsterdam Airport Schiphol, trains run every few minutes to Amsterdam Centraal station in about 15 to 20 minutes. From Centraal, the hotel is about 20 minutes on foot, ten minutes by tram towards Westermarkt, or a short taxi ride. A taxi from Schiphol to the hotel takes about 25 to 35 minutes. The canal-side street is narrow and one-way; taxis can stop briefly at the door of TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring to drop off guests and bags.

## TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring — Check-in and Check-out
Check-in at TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring is from 15:00 and check-out is by 11:00. Late check-out can be requested, subject to availability, which is limited in a hotel of this size. Reception, in the middle house, is staffed from 7:00 to 23:00; outside those hours a night manager is on call by phone and guests enter with their room key. Guests expecting to arrive after 23:00 should tell the hotel in advance. An identity document is required. Reservations can be cancelled free of charge up to 7 days before arrival.

## TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring — Room Categories
TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring offers four room categories. The Essential Room is about 20 square metres with a queen bed and a courtyard view over the hidden garden, for up to 2 guests. The Deluxe City View is about 28 square metres with a king bed and a sofa bed, and a city view over the canal, its bridges and the gabled houses opposite, for up to 3 guests.

The Family Studio is about 38 square metres, two connecting rooms with a kitchenette, for up to 4 guests. The Skyline Suite is about 55 square metres on the top floor under the old beams, with a private terrace above the rooftops and church towers, for up to 4 guests. The maximum is 4 guests per room at TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring, and children count as guests. Prices are not listed here; they come from the booking system.

## TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring — Breakfast
The breakfast buffet at TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring is included in every rate and served from 7:30 to 10:30 in the garden room of Tafel Zeven. It is mostly organic and local: dark and seeded breads, Dutch cheeses, smoked fish, boiled eggs, yoghurt with fruit, home-made granola, apple pie and, in true Dutch fashion, chocolate sprinkles for bread. Vegan and gluten-free options are available; with only 38 rooms, the kitchen can adapt to most diets if told the day before.

## TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring — Restaurants and Bars
Tafel Zeven is the restaurant of TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring, a small dining room of 30 seats with a fixed seasonal menu of four or six courses, largely vegetarian with North Sea fish, and a vegan version on request. Dinner is served from 18:00 to 21:30 from Wednesday to Sunday; the restaurant is closed on Mondays and Tuesdays. Reservations are essential.

De Stille Brug is the hotel bar, a wood-panelled front room on the canal, open from 12:00 to 23:00, specialising in Dutch genever, local beers and bar snacks such as cheese and croquettes. TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring has no room service, though drinks and snacks can be carried up from the bar.

## TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring — Fitness, Wellness and Pool
TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring has no swimming pool, no gym and no spa; the canal houses are too small and too old for them. Instead, the hotel lends bicycles free of charge, with helmets and child seats, and provides yoga mats in the rooms on request.

## TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring — Meetings and Events
TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring has one meeting space, the Regentenkamer, a canal-front room with a long oak table for up to 12 people, a screen and video-conferencing. The hidden garden can host private drinks for up to 30 guests until 21:00, and the hotel's boat can be hired for meetings on the water for up to eight people. ## TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring — Families and Children
Children are welcome at TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring, but parents should know its limits: the stairs are steep, the canal in front of the door is unfenced, and there is no pool or play area. The Family Studio, for up to 4 guests including children, is on a lower floor served by a lift. Cots and high chairs are free on request, and bicycles with child seats and a cargo bike can be borrowed.

## TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring — Pets
Small dogs up to about 10 kilograms are welcome at TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring, one per room, for a cleaning supplement per stay that reception confirms. Because of the steep stairs, dogs are placed in rooms on the lower floors. They are welcome in De Stille Brug and in the garden on a lead, but not in Tafel Zeven or on the boat. Assistance animals are always welcome, and the hotel will assign a suitable room.

## TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring — Parking
TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring has no parking, and the hotel advises against bringing a car into the canal ring, where streets are narrow and public parking is scarce and expensive. Guests arriving by car can use one of the park-and-ride sites on the edge of the city and continue by tram or metro, or a public garage about ten minutes away on foot; reception can explain both options.

## TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring — Accessibility
Accessibility at TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring is limited by the historic buildings, and the hotel prefers to be clear about it. The entrance of the middle house has a ramp, and lifts exist in two of the five houses only; rooms in the other three houses are reached by steep, narrow stairs. Two accessible rooms on the ground floor of the middle house have wide doors and adapted bathrooms with level showers. The bar, the restaurant and the garden are step-free from the middle house. The Skyline Suites are reached by lift plus a final short staircase. Guests with reduced mobility should call before booking.

## TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring — Wi-Fi and In-Room Amenities
Wi-Fi is free in all five houses of TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring and in the garden. Rooms have a smart television, a safe, a kettle with tea and filter coffee, a small fridge with complimentary water, a hairdryer, bathrobes, refillable bath products and thick curtains. Most rooms are cooled by ceiling fans and opening windows; only the Skyline Suites have air conditioning. Sockets are the European type at 230 volts.

## TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring — Nearby Sights and Neighbourhood Tips
The Anne Frank House and the Westerkerk are about five minutes on foot from TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring; tickets for the Anne Frank House are sold online only and sell out weeks ahead. The Nine Streets, with their small independent shops, start a few steps away, and the Jordaan district, with its cafés and Saturday market at the Noordermarkt, lies just across the canal. Dam Square and the Royal Palace are about ten minutes on foot. The Rijksmuseum and the Van Gogh Museum are about 25 minutes on foot or 15 minutes by tram. The hotel's electric boat, the Otter, leaves the private jetty for one-hour canal tours at 11:00 and 17:00 for up to eight guests; places are reserved at reception and charged separately.

## TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring — Good to Know
TelnyxHotel Amsterdam Canal Ring is strictly non-smoking in all houses, in the garden and on the suite terraces; this includes cannabis and electronic cigarettes, and a cleaning charge applies if the rule is broken. Quiet hours are from 22:00 to 7:30, as sound travels easily in old wooden houses, and the garden closes at 21:00. Luggage storage is free on arrival and departure days. Amsterdam charges a tourist tax calculated as a percentage of the room rate; it is added to the bill, and reception confirms the current percentage. Prices are quoted in euros.
