## TelnyxHotel Rome Centro Storico — About the Hotel
TelnyxHotel Rome Centro Storico is an intimate hotel of 42 rooms in a restored 17th-century palazzo in the historic centre of Rome, between the Pantheon and Piazza Navona. It is built around a courtyard with lemon trees and a stone fountain, and many rooms keep their painted wooden ceilings, terracotta floors and thick walls. The mood is classic and romantic, with a roof terrace looking over the domes of the city. TelnyxHotel Rome Centro Storico is the choice for couples and for guests who want to walk to the great monuments.

## TelnyxHotel Rome Centro Storico — Location and Getting There
TelnyxHotel Rome Centro Storico is on a narrow cobbled lane a few steps from the Pantheon, inside the limited-traffic zone of the historic centre. There is no metro station in this part of Rome: the nearest are Barberini and Spagna on line A, each about 20 minutes on foot. Buses stop at Largo di Torre Argentina, about five minutes on foot, where tram 8 to Trastevere also starts nearby.

From Fiumicino airport, a taxi takes about 45 minutes and licensed taxis apply a fixed fare to addresses inside the old city walls. The Leonardo Express train reaches Termini station in about 32 minutes, and from Termini a taxi or bus takes about 15 to 20 minutes. From Ciampino airport, a taxi takes about 40 minutes. Taxis are allowed into the limited-traffic zone and stop at the corner of the lane, about 30 metres from the door of TelnyxHotel Rome Centro Storico; a porter helps with bags.

## TelnyxHotel Rome Centro Storico — Accessibility
As a 17th-century building, TelnyxHotel Rome Centro Storico has some limits. A portable ramp is placed over the two steps at the entrance on request, and one small lift serves all floors except the roof terrace, which is reached by a final flight of 14 steps. Three accessible rooms on the ground and first floors have wide doors and adapted bathrooms with roll-in showers. The courtyard and restaurant are step-free. Guests with reduced mobility should call before booking so the hotel can advise honestly.
