## TelnyxHotel Madrid Gran Vía — About the Hotel
TelnyxHotel Madrid Gran Vía is a lively city hotel of 180 rooms in a grand 1920s building just off the Gran Vía, the theatre and shopping avenue of central Madrid. Its theme is the stage: a lobby with a red velvet curtain, corridors hung with invented vintage playbills and a rooftop with a seasonal pool and a busy bar above the city lights. TelnyxHotel Madrid Gran Vía keeps late hours, like Madrid itself, and suits guests who come for musicals, shopping, tapas and nightlife.

## TelnyxHotel Madrid Gran Vía — Location and Getting There
TelnyxHotel Madrid Gran Vía is on a side street a few steps from the Gran Vía, between the Callao and Gran Vía metro stations. Gran Vía station, on lines 1 and 5, and Callao station, on lines 3 and 5, are each about three minutes on foot. Puerta del Sol, with more metro lines and the suburban trains, is about seven minutes on foot.

From Adolfo Suárez Madrid-Barajas airport, a taxi takes about 25 to 35 minutes, with a flat fare to the city centre. By metro, line 8 runs from the airport to Nuevos Ministerios, where guests change towards Gran Vía, about 45 to 50 minutes in total. The 24-hour airport express bus reaches Plaza de Cibeles in about 40 minutes, from where it is a 15-minute walk or a short taxi ride. Atocha station, for high-speed trains, is about ten minutes by taxi, and Chamartín station about 20 minutes. Taxis stop at the door of TelnyxHotel Madrid Gran Vía.

TelnyxHotel Madrid Gran Vía has four room categories. ## TelnyxHotel Madrid Gran Vía — Fitness, Wellness and Pool
TelnyxHotel Madrid Gran Vía has an outdoor rooftop pool, open from June to mid-September, from 10:00 to 20:00. It is a small plunge pool for cooling off rather than for swimming lengths, reserved for hotel guests, with sunbeds available on a first-come basis. The pool is closed for the rest of the year, while the rooftop bar stays open all year with heaters. A gym on the second floor is open 24 hours with the room key. ## TelnyxHotel Madrid Gran Vía — Families and Children
Families are welcome at TelnyxHotel Madrid Gran Vía, although the hotel is lively at night. Courtyard-facing rooms and the Family Studio, which sleeps up to 4 guests including children, are the quietest. Cots and high chairs are free on request. Children may use the rooftop pool until 18:00 with an adult; after 20:00 the rooftop bar admits adults only. Entreacto has a children's menu at lunchtime. ## TelnyxHotel Madrid Gran Vía — Pets
Pets are not accepted at TelnyxHotel Madrid Gran Vía, as the busy location and the rooftop are not suitable for animals. Assistance animals are the exception and are welcome everywhere at no charge. Guests travelling with a dog in Madrid are welcome at TelnyxHotel Madrid Retiro, next to the park.

## TelnyxHotel Madrid Gran Vía — Parking
TelnyxHotel Madrid Gran Vía has no garage of its own. A valet service takes cars to a public underground car park about five minutes away, charged per night; the front desk confirms the rate. Central Madrid is a low-emission zone where access for private cars is restricted, so guests who drive must give their number plate to the front desk on arrival so that the car park can register the vehicle.

## TelnyxHotel Madrid Gran Vía — Accessibility
TelnyxHotel Madrid Gran Vía has a step-free side entrance next to the main revolving door, and five lifts reach every floor, including the rooftop. Nine accessible rooms have wide doors, roll-in showers and grab bars, and some are connected to a standard room for companions. The rooftop bar is accessible, and the plunge pool has a hoist. ## TelnyxHotel Madrid Gran Vía — Nearby Sights and Neighbourhood Tips
The theatres and big shops of the Gran Vía are on the doorstep of TelnyxHotel Madrid Gran Vía. Puerta del Sol is about seven minutes on foot and the Plaza Mayor about twelve. The Royal Palace and the Almudena Cathedral are about 15 minutes away, and the Temple of Debod, a favourite sunset spot, about 15 minutes to the west. The Malasaña and Chueca neighbourhoods, just north of the avenue, are full of small bars and independent shops. The Prado Museum is about 20 minutes on foot or a few minutes by taxi. Madrid eats late: restaurants fill from 21:30. The avenue is crowded on weekend evenings, so guests should keep an eye on bags and phones.
