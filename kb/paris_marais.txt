## TelnyxHotel Paris Le Marais — Location and Getting There
TelnyxHotel Paris Le Marais is on a side street a few steps from the Place des Vosges, in the heart of Le Marais. ## TelnyxHotel Paris Le Marais — Accessibility

## TelnyxHotel Paris Le Marais — Nearby Sights and Neighbourhood Tips
The Place des Vosges, the oldest planned square in Paris, is a few steps from TelnyxHotel Paris Le Marais. The Musée Picasso and the Musée Carnavalet, which tells the history of Paris, are each about five to ten minutes on foot. The old Jewish quarter around the Rue des Rosiers is five minutes away and good for a quick lunch. The Île Saint-Louis is about ten minutes on foot and Notre-Dame about 15. The Louvre is about 25 minutes on foot or four stops on line 1. Many boutiques in Le Marais open on Sundays, unlike much of Paris, so Sunday afternoons are crowded.
